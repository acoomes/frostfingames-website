Privacy

What we keep, and what we don’t.

The short version: if you give us your email address, we keep it so we can tell you about our games. That is the only thing this site collects on purpose.

Last updated November 26, 2026. This page covers frostfingames.com.

Who we are

Frostfin Games is an independent game studio in Portland, Oregon. For anything on this page, write to hello@frostfingames.com.

If you join the email list

When you submit one of the signup forms, we store:

  • your email address;
  • which form you used, so we know which game you asked about;
  • when you first signed up and when you last did;
  • the country your request came from, as reported by our host. We do not store your IP address.

We use this to send you news about Frostfin games, and for nothing else. We don’t sell the list, share it or hand it to advertisers.

To get off the list, or to see or correct what we hold about you, email hello@frostfingames.com from the address you signed up with and we’ll take care of it. Once we start sending email, every message will also carry an unsubscribe link.

If you email us

We receive your message and whatever you put in it, and we keep it for as long as the conversation is useful. We don’t add you to the list because you wrote to us.

What this site doesn’t do

  • No analytics, no advertising trackers and no social media pixels.
  • No cookies set by us.
  • No accounts, so no passwords or profiles.

Perfect Pulse, the small game hosted on this site, saves your high score in your own browser. It never leaves your device, and clearing your browser’s site data removes it.

Who else handles the data

The site and the signup list are hosted by Cloudflare, and our email is routed through Cloudflare to our inbox provider. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes technical details of each request, such as your IP address and browser type, to deliver pages and block abuse. We don’t use that information to identify visitors.

Links to other places

Some links lead off this site, including Paper 86 at paper86.app, game prototypes hosted elsewhere, and YouTube. Those are separate sites with their own practices, and this page doesn’t cover them.

Children

The email list is meant for adults. We don’t knowingly collect email addresses from children under 13. If you think a child has signed up, tell us and we’ll delete the address.

Changes

If what we collect changes, this page changes first and the date at the top moves with it.
