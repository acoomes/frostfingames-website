Flagship · Play it now

PAPER 86

A 60-second drift score attack on a sheet of paper.

Throw the car sideways, skim past the cones without touching them, and straighten out to bank your points before a wall wipes them out. Every run ends with a print of the ink you laid down.

  • Twenty events. Ten circuits, each also driven in reverse, with five medals apiece from bronze to diamond.
  • A fresh sheet every day. A daily circuit generated from the date: the same for everyone, gone tomorrow.
  • Arcade-style boards. A top 10 for every event and every daily sheet, and your best run comes back as a ghost to beat.
  • Play your way. Keyboard, touch or gamepad, at your desk or on your phone.

Cookiss

A cozy cookie clicker with a hamster problem.

Tap the cookie. Buy the upgrades. Feed Cookie the Hamster. Come back later to a pile of cookies you earned while you were away. Cookiss is a comfy idle game built to be the calmest five minutes of your day.

  • Welcome-back cookies. Offline earnings keep the oven warm while you live your life.
  • Achievements that pay. Every milestone stacks a permanent bonus.
  • Nothing predatory. No forced ads, no energy timers, no loot boxes. Promise.

Shelf Control

Tap a cat. Build the picture. Don’t run out of boxes.

Cats stroll along a shelf and bat coloured blocks off it. Every block that lands builds a pixel-art picture from the floor up. Cats with leftovers nap in cardboard boxes, and when the boxes run out, so do you. Forty hand-curated levels plus a daily puzzle.

Grow a hamlet at the edge of the dark, send a party below and watch the fights play out. Heroes level up, gear up, carry their wounds and die for good. Resurrection exists. It is not cheap.

The studio

Fun is the business. Here’s how we run it.

01

Fun first, fair always.

No forced ads, no energy timers, no loot boxes, no pay-to-skip. If a Frostfin game earns money, it’s because you liked it enough to want more of it.

02

Small sessions, deep hooks.

Games you can finish a round of on a coffee break and still be thinking about at dinner. Easy to learn, stubborn to master.

03

Prototype fast, ship on evidence.

Every game starts as a playable question. The ones that answer “yes” get the full treatment. The ones that don’t teach us something first.

Frostfin Games is an independent studio in Portland, Oregon, founded by Andrew Coomes: product builder, former race car driver and lifelong nerd. We build lean, ship often, and play everything we make far more than is strictly healthy.
