Cookiss

A cozy cookie clicker with a hamster problem.

Tap the cookie. Buy the upgrades. Feed Cookie the Hamster. Come back later to a pile of cookies you earned while you were away. Cookiss is a kawaii idle game built to be the calmest five minutes of your day.

Shelf Control

Tap a cat. Build the picture. Don’t run out of boxes.

Cats stroll along a shelf and bat coloured blocks off it. Every block that lands builds a pixel-art picture from the floor up. Cats with leftovers nap in cardboard boxes, and when the boxes run out, so do you. Forty hand-curated levels plus a daily puzzle.

The studio

Fun is the business. Here’s how we run it.

01

Fun first, fair always.

No forced ads, no energy timers, no loot boxes, no pay-to-skip. If a Frostfin game earns money, it’s because you liked it enough to want more of it.

02

Small sessions, deep hooks.

Games you can finish a round of on a coffee break and still be thinking about at dinner. Easy to learn, stubborn to master.

03

Prototype fast, ship on evidence.

Every game starts as a playable question. The ones that answer “yes” get the full treatment. The ones that don’t teach us something first.

Frostfin Games is an independent studio in Portland, Oregon, founded by Andrew Coomes: product builder, former race car driver and lifelong nerd. We build lean, ship often, and play everything we make far more than is strictly healthy.
